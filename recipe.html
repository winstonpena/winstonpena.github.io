Moms Oatmeal Cookies

The origins of oatmeal cookies can be explored back to the first recorded recipes that emerged in early American cookbooks. These recipes, dating back to the late 1800s, marked the beginning of the delicious history of oatmeal cookies. The traditional recipe includes oats, brown sugar, butter, and flour with tasty variations such as raisins or chocolate chips.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup shortening
  • 2 cups brown sugar
  • 2 eggs, well beaten
  • 1 teaspoon soda
  • 1/4 cup boiling water
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 2 cups sifted flour
  • 2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats

Directions:

  1. Add eggs
  2. Dissolve soda in water and add to mixture
  3. Add vanilla
  4. Mix flour with oats and add
  5. Mix all together and drop by teaspoons on greased cookie sheet
  6. Bake at 400 degrees, 15 minutes
