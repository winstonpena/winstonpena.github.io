Winston Pena

Hi, I am Winston Pena. I am a 14 year old Mexican American boy living in Chicago and a student at Lane Tech College Prep. I play basketball and hope to make the team. I’ve played for many travel teams and for my middle school, Chappell Eliza Elementary.

Being Mexican involves a rich tapestry of traditions, family values, and cultural pride. For me, this identity is tied to family, food, and a blend of indigenous and Spanish influences that shape my values.
